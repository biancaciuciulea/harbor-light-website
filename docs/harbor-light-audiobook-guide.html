How Harbor audio maps

Listeners compare products. Here is how our studio path sits next to the formats people already know — without pretending we are Audible or GraphicAudio.

  • What we do not sell. A flat single-voice TTS dump of the manuscript, or a Kindle Text-to-Speech pass sold as an audiobook. That flattens dual-lead tension and is not the Harbor Soft Hour product.
  • What listeners often compare us to. Dual-narrator Audible editions (two voices, usually light SFX) and full-cast dramatizations (cast + music + effects). Useful reference — different storefronts and credit models.
  • What Harbor Light Book 1 is. Studio cinematic pack on Gumroad: 24 chapters with score and SFX under the performance. Free Spotify chapters are the sample lane. Kindle is the separate ebook lane. One author studio · three platforms · one series.
  • Romanian YouTube (separate product). Free Edge Alina RO uploads on YouTube (Books 1–2, two parts each) are a discovery funnel for Romanian listeners. Honest claim: Edge TTS + burned subtitles — not AVA, not the Gumroad Cinematic Soft Hour EN pack.

Heat & darkness scale

Clear axes beat a vague “dark romance” label. Harbor Light Book 1 sits here:

  • Burn — Slow. Devotion builds under protocol; not instalove.
  • Heat — Adult. On-page intimacy. Not fade-to-black contemporary.
  • Darkness — Mafia / leverage. Empire protocol, power imbalance, captivity-adjacent tension. Not cozy.
  • HEA — Earned, still costs. Ending lands — loyalty still has a price.

How we make Harbor audio

Author-studio method — the opposite of a sponsored “best of” list.
