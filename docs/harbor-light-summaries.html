Book 1

Bound by Blood and Harbor Light

Three locks. An unsigned marriage pact. A Shadow Hand who measures before he speaks.

Free Ch1 text · Spotify · Gumroad Soft Hour (24 ch) · Kindle EN/DE/FR/ES/IT

Elena Voss has counted every lock in the warded room and every clause the Obsidian Coven wants signed by dawn. Julian Kane — steel eyes, twelve years without a public failure — is not here to negotiate. The document wants her signature, her harbor districts, and an initiation no one will describe until morning. He does not flinch. She does not surrender. In mist-shrouded Harbor Light, loyalty is currency — and love has no line in the ledger.

Book 1 is the arranged-pact ignition: slow burn, captivity-adjacent protocol, and the first crack in a man built from orders. Start here — free Chapter 1 on this site, Spotify sample, full Soft Hour cinematic on Gumroad, novel on Kindle.

Book 2

Bound by Soft Marks and Harbor Light

The ledger already has his name. Quiet List mark. Glass Holding stamp. Berths — or invisibility.

Kindle EN/DE/FR/ES/IT · Gumroad Soft Hour EN (23 ch) · Spotify trailer EN · free RO Edge YouTube (2 parts)

Someone wedged the parchment between the safehouse floorboards while Julian Kane checked the south jetty. Harbor seal. Quiet List mark. Glass Holding's secondary stamp in the fold. Charges written as corporate language for grain diverted to orphanages — theft, by their accounting. What they want: berths, and Julian made invisible if he refuses.

Elena has weeks of Holding documentation and a mark that wants to go brass. Julian has unauthorized work and the stillness of a man who saw this coming. When protocol becomes a kill order, devotion is the only currency left that doesn't belong to the syndicate. Book 2 turns the system into the villain — paper faster than knives.

If Book 1 hooked you on the pact, Book 2 shows what the empire does when loyalty becomes a line item.

  • Quiet List / ledger pressure
  • System as villain
  • Established tension after the pact
  • Berth leverage
  • Slow-burn heat through protocol

Book 3

Bound by Soft Hour and Harbor Light

The clinic opens on their time. The harbor wants it back.

Kindle EN

Under Soft Hour light, Elena Voss and Julian Kane hold a basement triage floor while Glass Holding prices mercy in blood and paperwork. Soft Hour is not a romance backdrop — it is triage under empire. Elena still counts exits when the door scrapes wrong. Julian stills his hands when collectors smile. Holding paper moves faster than knives. Pharmacy routes become choke points. Loyalty seals decide who gets gauze — and who gets a visit.

They are already each other's. The harbor still wants a price. Book 3 is established couple, medical competence as leverage (not a genre swap), and HEA pressure with the cost still visible.

If you want dark mafia romance where care is contraband and the Soft Hour clinic is the battleground.

  • Established couple
  • Soft Hour clinic under siege
  • Glass Holding as system
  • Slow-burn heat
  • HEA with visible cost

Book 4

Bound by Soft Gate and Harbor Light

Glass Holding is starving Soft Hour. Soft Gate turns sealed history into a weapon.

Kindle EN

Hale's gray coats turn sealed charts into pharmacy squeeze and berth denial. Elena and Julian — already each other's — keep the basement clinic open under Holding paper while Soft Gate converts medical history into leverage aimed at the people they refuse to abandon. Mira's name on the chalk board is a target Holding can read. Soft Hour is a triage floor under siege, and love is the ledger line they will not surrender.
