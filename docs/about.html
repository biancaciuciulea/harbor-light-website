About the author

Picu Bianca-Maria writes Harbor Light, an adult dark mafia romance series set in a misted harbor where loyalty is currency and love has no line in the ledger. Elena Voss (dark eyes) and Julian Kane (steel eyes) lead a slow-burn arc across Books 1-4 - arranged-pact tension, protocol, and HEA that still costs. Start with Bound by Blood and Harbor Light.

She publishes alone. Alongside Harbor Light she writes practical AI and LLM engineering books for real work, and as Picu Bianca she releases 9D House music for headphones.
