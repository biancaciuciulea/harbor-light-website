# Offline Book Copilot — Study Any PDF or EPUB on Your Own Machine

You have forty PDFs you meant to read. Textbooks, papers, manuals, that certification guide. You've opened three of them.

The problem isn't discipline. It's that reading a 400-page technical PDF gives you no way to check what stayed. You finish a chapter, and a week later you couldn't say what was in it.

This is a desktop app that turns any PDF or EPUB into something you can actually work through.

Drop a book in. Then:

Ask it questions and get the answer quoted from the book, with the page and a confidence score. Generate a chapter summary. Get a quiz built from the book's own terminology. Build flashcards with spaced repetition that schedules your reviews and exports straight to Anki. Sit a timed exam and see which chapters you're weak on. Have any chapter read aloud, or export it as an audiobook MP3.

Everything runs on your computer.

---

## It quotes. It doesn't invent.

This matters enough to say plainly, because it's the opposite of what most AI tools do.

Ask a question and you get the actual passages from your book, with sources and a confidence percentage. Not a paraphrase. Not a generated summary of what the model thinks the book probably said.

If the book doesn't contain the answer, it tells you it doesn't contain the answer.

There is no language model writing prose here, which means there is nothing that can hallucinate a fact, misattribute a quote, or confidently invent a statistic. When you're studying for something that matters, that's not a limitation — it's the whole point.

If you want an AI that writes essays about your book, this isn't it. If you want one that shows you exactly what your book says and helps you retain it, keep reading.

---

## No subscription. No API keys. No cloud.

One payment. No OpenAI key, no Anthropic key, no Ollama, no account.

Your books never leave your computer. Your notes, your progress, your uploads — all local files you can back up or delete.

The internet is used for exactly three optional things: the read-aloud voice, speech-to-text if you ask questions by microphone, and translation if you turn it on. Turn those off and it runs with the wifi disconnected.

## Installs in about ten minutes

Windows — double-click the installer, then double-click to start

macOS — same, two double-clicks

Linux — one command

Docker — one command, if you prefer

If Python is missing, the installer tells you exactly what to do. On Windows it can install it for you. Everything lives inside one folder; delete the folder and it's gone.

There's a 30-second check that confirms it works before you rely on it.

## Honestly, who this is not for

If your PDFs are scanned images, text extraction won't work — this needs real text, not pictures of text. DRM-protected files won't open either.

If you want generated prose, essays, or an AI that writes for you, buy something else. This one quotes.

If you won't spend ten minutes installing something, the browser tools are easier. They also upload your books to someone else's server and charge monthly.
