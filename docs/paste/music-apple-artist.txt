## Artist bio (paste into Apple Music for Artists / site)

Picu Bianca makes 9D House and spatial electronic for headphones. Earliest memory: sitting still with oversized headphones, watching sound turn into visual emotion and another world. Turning point: hearing the first mastered 9D track and knowing listeners can feel completely enveloped as sound moves around them. Featured track: Citylight Surrender (from Iron Pulse Rising) — late-night cinematic 9D; close your eyes and get lost in it. Influences: Daft Punk (Discovery / Random Access Memories) for timeless electronic warmth, and Aphex Twin (Selected Ambient Works) for pure soundscape. Bucket list: push 9D further with visual artists into fully immersive, synchronized installations. Same person as author Picu Bianca-Maria. Hub: https://picubiancamaria.com/music.html
