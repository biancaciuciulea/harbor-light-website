<h2>Mastering Large Language Models: From Theory to Production: Complete Guide for Developers, Data Scientists, and AI Engineers</h2><p>&quot;Mastering Large Language Models: From Theory to Production&quot; is the definitive guide for anyone who wants to deeply understand and implement large language models in production. Written by Picu Bianca-Maria, this book offers a &quot;Senior Architect&quot; perspective on the entire LLM lifecycle, from theoretical foundations to complex production implementations. The book covers all essential aspects: from Transformer architecture and pre-training, to fine-tuning, prompt engineering, RAG, optimization, deployment, scaling, and security. Each chapter is an original synthesis, based on a vast bibliography, but reformulated with unique analogies and critical comparisons.</p><h3>What you will learn</h3><ul><li>Foundations: Deep understanding of Transformer architecture and language model evolution</li><li>Training: Pre-training and fine-tuning, including PEFT techniques like LoRA and QLoRA</li><li>Prompt Engineering: From fundamentals to advanced techniques (chain-of-thought, tree-of-thought)</li><li>RAG: Implementing Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems</li><li>Optimization: Quantization, pruning, distillation for efficiency</li><li>Deployment: Deployment strategies, API design, testing</li><li>Scaling: Distributed training and inference</li><li>Monitoring: Observability, logging, alerting for LLM systems</li><li>Cost Management: Cost optimization and resource management</li><li>Security: Protection against attacks, prompt injection, best practices</li><li>Evaluation: Metrics, benchmarks, A/B testing</li><li>Case Studies: Real-world implementations and lessons learned</li><li>Future: Emerging trends and new architectures</li></ul><p><b>This book is the essential resource for developers, data scientists, and AI engineers who want to build robust, scalable, and efficient LLM systems.</b></p>
