Entities

Elena Voss · Lead
Heir of House Voss. Dark eyes. Counts locks, clauses, and exits. Forced into an arranged marriage pact with the Obsidian Coven — she reads the trap before she signs.
Julian Kane · Lead
Shadow Hand of the Obsidian Coven. Steel eyes. Twelve years without a public failure. Morally grey MMC — protocol first, devotion that costs.
Obsidian Coven · Faction
Harbor syndicate power. Patriarch at the top; Shadow Hand enforcement; marriage pacts and district absorption as administrative weapons.
Harbor Light (city) · Setting
Misted harbor city where loyalty is currency. Ward-stone rooms, berths, docks, and paper that moves faster than knives.
Quiet List · System (Book 2+)
Ledger mark / pressure system. Charges written as corporate language. Refusal can mean berths — or invisibility.
Glass Holding · System (Book 2+)
Corporate-facing Holding power inside the empire. Stamps, pharmacy routes, loyalty seals, and paperwork that prices mercy.
Soft Hour · Motif / clinic (Books 3–4)
Not a romance backdrop — a basement triage floor under empire. Care as leverage. On this site, Cinematic Soft Hour also names the Gumroad audio format (SFX + score).
Soft Gate · Pressure (Book 4)
Holding converts sealed medical history into a weapon. Named without spoiling the full mechanism — revealed in-story.
The Patriarch · Antagonist pressure
Doesn't ask — draws the contract. Wants the Voss name and the appearance of continuity in the harbor districts.
Corvus · Network
Surveillance / enforcement network under Coven hierarchy. Procedural pressure and watch posts around Elena and Julian.
