What is 9D music by Picu Bianca?

9D here means headphone-first spatial production: the mix is engineered so elements move around the listener in stereo (left/right depth), not flat mono-center electronic. Apple Music lists the artist under House. All Picu Bianca DistroKid / Apple Music releases use this 9D approach. Start with Citylight Surrender from Iron Pulse Rising, or the curated playlists named Picu Bianca-Maria on Apple Music and Spotify. Put headphones on — that is the intended playback.
