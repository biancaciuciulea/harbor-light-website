Who is Picu Bianca-Maria?

Picu Bianca-Maria is an independent author and digital creator. She publishes the Harbor Light dark mafia romance series, DistroKid music, and practical AI & tech Kindle books. This site (picubiancamaria.com) is the official hub.

Free trailer and chapter episodes are on Spotify (podcast) where live — English, German, French, Spanish, and Italian Book 1 shows. Start from the language landing or the Harbor Light series page.

Where is the full cinematic audiobook?

The complete 24-chapter cinematic audiobook (audio-only) is sold on Gumroad — not as an ebook. Buy the language edition you want, then listen offline.

Where can I read the Harbor Light novels on Kindle?

Harbor Light ebooks are on Amazon Kindle (KDP). Site CTAs use amazon.com for every language edition. The novel is not sold as an ebook on Gumroad.

Are romance, music, and AI books the same catalog?

No. Harbor Light is dark mafia romance. Music is DistroKid. AI & Tech Books are nonfiction Kindle titles with optional Gumroad companions. They share one author hub but stay in separate catalogs.

What languages is Harbor Light Book 1 available in?

Book 1 Kindle is live in English, German, French, Spanish, and Italian. Cinematic Gumroad audio is live for those languages. Free Spotify chapters are live for EN/DE/FR and growing for ES/IT.
