Picu Bianca-Maria is an independent author and digital creator. She publishes the Harbor Light dark mafia romance series, DistroKid music, and practical AI & tech Kindle books. This site (picubiancamaria.com) is the official hub.

Are romance, music, and AI books the same catalog?

No. Harbor Light is dark mafia romance. Music is DistroKid. AI & Tech Books are nonfiction Kindle titles with optional Gumroad companions. They share one author hub but stay in separate catalogs.

What kind of romance is Harbor Light?

Harbor Light is adult dark mafia romance (romantasy-adjacent harbor setting): slow burn, morally grey MMC (Julian Kane), arranged marriage pact tension, and HEA that still costs. It is not sweet contemporary romance and not a tech book.

What is the difference between Spotify free chapters and the Gumroad audiobook?

Spotify hosts free trailer/chapter podcast episodes so readers can sample the voice. Gumroad sells the complete cinematic 24-chapter audiobook (audio-only, with SFX/music). Kindle is the ebook. Official path: free Spotify → full Gumroad audio → Kindle novel.
