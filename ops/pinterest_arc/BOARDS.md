## Ongoing rhythm (weeks 3+)

- **Mon:** Series or Recruit  
- **Wed:** Book pin (rotate 1→2→3→4)  
- **Fri:** Trope badge  
- **+2 flexible:** A/B title tests on best-performing image  
- Never more than 1 pin/hour; prefer morning EU / evening US Eastern for romance traffic

## Tracking

1. Use the exact UTM links from `LINK_MATRIX.md` / `PIN_COPY.md`.  
2. In GA4 (site already has GTM): filter `utm_campaign = arc_harbor`.  
3. Compare `utm_content`: `series` vs `book1` vs `recruit` vs tropes.  
4. After 14 days: double down on the top 2 `utm_content` winners; pause losers.  
5. ARC form submissions: check Formspree / email subject lines (`ARC Team — …`) + sheet if you use `ops/ARC_GOOGLE_SHEETS_SETUP.md`.

---

## Conversion rules (do not break)

1. Destination is always an **ARC** URL — never Amazon as the pin link.  
2. Say **slow burn / protocol before heat** in description.  
3. Say **honest reviews / stars not for sale**.  
4. 18+ content notes when space allows.  
5. One job per pin: join ARC — not “buy + listen + Spotify” clutter.
